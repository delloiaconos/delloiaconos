# I'm Salvatore Dello Iacono


**Embedded Systems Engineer | Instrumentation & Measurements Engineer | Industrial Vision**

**Assistant Professor (RTD-A) at the Department of Information Engineering, University of Brescia**


I work at the intersection of **embedded systems**, **electronic measurements** and **industrial systems** combining academic research with hands-on design, firmware development, laboratory validation, and industrial-oriented prototyping.

My activities focus on the design and validation of **real-time measurement and control systems**, with experience ranging from microcontroller-based firmware and FPGA prototyping to industrial vision algorithms, uncertainty-aware measurement software, MEMS-based positioning, and Battery Management Systems.



## About Me

* Assistant Professor at the **Department of Information Engineering, University of Brescia** | Brescia (BS), Italy.
* Ph.D. in **Industrial Engineering – Electronics / Instrumentation & Measurements**.
* Lecturer of **3D Vision Systems** at the University of Brescia.
* Qualified as **Associate Professor** in the Italian higher education system, sector **09/E4 – Electrical and Electronic Engineering and Measurements**.
* Registered Professional Engineer in Information Engineering.
* IEEE Member, affiliated with IEEE IMS and IEEE IES.
* Mentor and tutor in embedded systems and hands-on engineering projects, including the **NeaPolis Innovation Summer Campus**.


## Current Interests

* Real-time embedded signal and image processing.
* Embedded measurement systems for industrial applications.
* 3D vision, industrial inspection, and metrological characterization of vision algorithms.
* Smart sensing, IIoT, and laboratory automation.
* Battery modeling, BMS architectures, and sustainable light electric mobility.

## Main Areas of Expertise

### Embedded Systems & Real-Time Firmware

* Firmware development for real-time and resource-constrained systems.
* ARM Cortex-based platforms and STM32 microcontrollers.
* Hardware/software co-design for measurement and control applications.
* Low-level interfacing with sensors, communication peripherals, and laboratory instrumentation.
* Development of test benches, validation procedures, and experimental setups.

### Measurement & Instrumentation

* Automated measurement systems for industrial and laboratory applications.
* Metrological characterization of algorithms, software, and measurement setups.
* Uncertainty evaluation, propagation, and optimization of complex measurement procedures.
* Design and validation of calibration procedures for electronic and industrial systems.

### Industrial Vision & 3D Vision

* Vision-based measurement systems for industrial applications.
* Classical image processing for measurement extraction.
* Embedded image processing on microcontrollers and single-board platforms.
* Metrological evaluation of vision algorithms and acquisition setups.
* 2D/3D vision systems for inspection, monitoring, and measurement tasks.

### Battery Management & Electric Mobility 

* Battery modeling using electrical equivalent circuits.
* State-of-Charge and State-of-Health estimation algorithms.
* Battery Management System architectures.
* Smart charging infrastructures and light electric mobility applications.
* Testing workflows and validation procedures for battery systems.

### MEMS, Sensor Fusion & Positioning

* Heading and orientation estimation using MEMS inertial sensors.
* Sensor data fusion algorithms for indoor positioning and navigation.
* Power-consumption optimization strategies for embedded positioning systems.
* Real-time signal processing on microcontrollers and DSP-oriented platforms.

### Digital Design & Hardware Prototyping

* FPGA-based digital circuit design and prototyping.
* VHDL development for measurement and control architectures.
* Mixed-signal electronic system design, schematic capture, and PCB layout.
* Laboratory validation with electronic and electrical measurement instrumentation.
